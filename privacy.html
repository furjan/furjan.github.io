Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. This explains what Quiet Garden Mahjong (Sessiz Bahçe) processes and why.

Controller: Furkan Aykaç. Contact: kayraaykac@hotmail.com.tr.

What we collect

  • Guest account: a random ID made on first launch. No name or email is asked.
  • Game save: level, stars, settings, player name, avatar and frame, kept so progress survives a phone change.
  • Friends: your friend code, friends list, duel results and daily time. Your name, avatar, level and daily time are visible to your friends only. If you are among the 10 fastest players of the daily puzzle, your name and time are visible to all players.
  • Usage measurement: level starts, wins, losses and levels left unfinished, ads watched, opening the purchase screen and purchases, invites and shares, helper use, launch time, smoothness, phone model, OS version and error logs, used to improve the game.
  • Push token: if you allow notifications, for duel alerts.
  • What you send us: messages from inside the game and players you report.
  • Linked account: if you sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook, that account ID and email.

A profile photo chosen from your gallery stays on your phone and is never uploaded.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and device data to the extent you allow. You can change your choices in the consent window and in your phone settings. Details: Google partner policy.

Who we share with

We do not sell your data. These providers run parts of the service: Supabase (database and accounts), Expo (notifications), Google (ads, sign-in), Apple and Google (stores, purchases), Facebook (if you choose it to sign in). Their servers may be outside your country.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. In the game, Profile > Delete my account and data removes everything for good. See account deletion.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to kayraaykac@hotmail.com.tr.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
