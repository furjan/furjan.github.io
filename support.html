Support

The quickest way: in the game, Settings > Write to us. You can also write to kayraaykac@hotmail.com.tr; we aim to reply within 3 working days.

Common questions

Can I lose my progress? Progress is saved to the cloud automatically. Before changing phones, link an Apple, Google or Facebook account in Profile.

How do I remove ads? Settings > Remove ads. It is one payment, not a subscription.

I paid but still see ads. Settings > Restore purchase.

I saw an inappropriate ad. Settings > Write to us > Inappropriate ad.

How do I report a player? Hold their name in your friends list and choose Report and block.
